Hi, I'm Ömer. I make games, mostly the part you can *feel*: controls, timing, physics, juice and the many small systems that make a game satisfying to play.

I've been doing this for more than 10 years. I started on the racing sim *RaceRoom* in Skövde, built an MMO pool game, VR and AR prototypes, simulators and a lot of mobile games in Ankara, co-founded **Polyfun Games** and shipped *Dynamic Shooting VR* and a Nintendo Switch port of *A Night at the Races*. Most recently I was a senior game programmer at **Resolution Games** in Stockholm, building multiplayer games for Meta Quest and Snap Spectacles.
