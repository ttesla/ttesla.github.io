A one-to-one port of a millisecond-sensitive Flash platformer to Nintendo Switch. The port had to play *exactly* like the original, or some levels would become impossible, so the platforming part needed a lot of care.

The adventure side had many Flash animations and sequences. I recreated them in Unity as video files and wrote tools to manage them.
