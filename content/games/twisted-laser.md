Classic laser-mirror puzzle game with a retro feel. Click the mirrors to rotate them until the laser beam hits all the pylons. Left and right click rotate in opposite directions.

I made it at Global Game Jam 2016, then kept adding levels and fixing bugs for 8 months before the final release.
